The **Vinewood** and **Cypress Flats** Ammu-Nations have a range in the back. Pay **{money:range.price}** at the counter for a **{var:range.minutes}-minute** lane, walk through the door and shoot the targets as they pop up.

- Pays **XP only** — no cash.
- Bring your own weapon and ammo.
- Shooters are protected from outside PvP while on the lane.
