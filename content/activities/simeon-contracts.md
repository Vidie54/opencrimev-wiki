Simeon Yetarian's dealership on the blip offers **contracts**: short PvE missions for one to four players. The police are **not** alerted by any of them.

| Contract | Time limit | Pays |
|---|---|---|
| **Repossess** | 10 min | $2,000–4,500 |
| **Vandalize** | 8 min | $1,200–2,200 |
| **Write-off** | 8 min | $1,800–3,000 |
| **Shakedown** | 6 min | $1,500–2,800 |

## How it works

- Start one at the lot. **Anyone within 15 m joins the group**, up to four.
- Each contract has its own objective — find and repossess a car, wreck one, deliver a write-off, collect from a debtor.
- The reward is paid to everyone within **80 m of the drop** at the end, multiplied by distance and crew size (up to ×2), plus XP.
- The time limit is the server's; the countdown on screen is only a display.
- `/cancelmission` abandons it.

A repossess or write-off car can be **diverted** into your own vehicle warehouse when running solo — see [Warehouses](wiki:properties/warehouses#cargo-from-jobs).
