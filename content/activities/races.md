Five hubs on the map: **street**, **bike**, **boat**, **heli** and **plane**. Walk up to one to open the lobby list, host a race or join one.

## Lobbies

- The host picks the track, laps (up to five) and the **buy-in** — up to **{money:races.maxBuyIn}**, paid straight into the pool by everyone who joins.
- The pool is split by finishing position: **70/30** with two placed, **60/25/15** with three, **50/25/15/10** with four or more.
- **DNF** after three minutes without a checkpoint.
- **XP**: 40 for a win, 15 for finishing.

## Protection

Racers cannot be damaged by anyone outside the race, and cannot damage them — the game refuses the hit. Ramming the grid from outside is [griefing](wiki:rules/combat#races-and-the-shooting-range-are-protected).
