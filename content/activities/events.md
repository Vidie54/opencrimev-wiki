World events run one at a time and are announced in chat. `/events` lists what is on.

## Airdrop

A military crate drops from the sky somewhere on the map, marked for everybody. First to open it takes a large cash prize — and everybody else knows where you are.

## Security truck

An armoured truck with guards runs a route. Two or more players, explosives on the back doors, take the case. See [Robberies](wiki:crime/robberies#security-truck).

## Seasonal

Weekend, Easter, Christmas and other global events multiply payouts for their duration — [Bonuses & events](wiki:economy/bonuses-and-events).
