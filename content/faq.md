## I died — where is my stuff?

**{var:drops.percent} % of your cash** and **one weapon per group** dropped where you died, for five minutes. The bank is untouched. Anything in a [weapon storage](wiki:properties/wardrobe-and-storage) is safe. You paid a **{money:respawn.deathFee}** hospital fee on respawn — a [paramedic revive](wiki:jobs/paramedic) costs nothing.

## How do I lose stars?

Evade for {var:wanted.evasionSeconds} seconds per star with no officer within {var:wanted.evasionRadius} m, buy something at a clothing store or barber, or turn yourself in at a station. Dying does not clear them. [Wanted level](wiki:crime/wanted-level).

## My car is gone.

In order: **My Vehicles → Impounded** (an officer took it — [bail or retrieve](wiki:crime/prison-and-impound)); **My Vehicles → Respawn** (destroyed or lost — it goes back to its garage); the garage lease ran out (extend it). [Owning a vehicle](wiki:vehicles/owning).

## I'm stuck in an interior.

Walk to the door and press {key:INPUT_PICKUP}. If the door is gone, **Interaction Menu → Kill Yourself** respawns you at a hospital (the fee applies). If that fails too, `/report` it — a staff member can move you.

## How do I link my account to the website or Discord?

**Interaction Menu → Account Info → Linked Accounts**. The website ({website}) shows your stats and sells VIP; Discord linking is how staff verify you on the server.

## What does VIP give?

+{var:vip.rewardBonus} % on every payout and −{var:vip.shopDiscount} % on every purchase, per account. [Bonuses & events](wiki:economy/bonuses-and-events).

## "Your name must match."

Your FiveM nickname and your character name have to be the same. You changed one of them — change it back, or change the other. [First login](wiki:getting-started/first-login).

## Can I be robbed while passive?

Other players cannot hurt you in passive mode or under New Player Protection, and you cannot pick up drops from fights you are protected from. Your cash still drops if you die some other way — bank it. [New Player Protection](wiki:getting-started/new-player-protection).

## Where do I report a bug or a player?

`/report` in game, or the Discord ({discord}). For a mistake on this wiki, use *Edit this page on GitHub* at the bottom.
