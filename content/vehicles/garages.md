## Buying

Garages are on the map by type — **land**, **heli**, **boat** — each with a slot count. Buy one from its marker. A garage is **leased for {var:garage.days} days**; extend it from the garage menu before it runs out, or the cars wait inside until you do.

The [starter pack](wiki:getting-started/starter-pack) includes a voucher for one free {var:starter.garageSlots}-slot land garage.

## Other parking

- A [house](wiki:properties/houses) has its own garage.
- A [vehicle warehouse](wiki:properties/warehouses) is a garage with tiers.
- Rentals have parking too.

## Invites

`/invite <id>` lets a nearby player into a property you own — the garage or the house; `/uninvite <id>` takes it back. Invited players can enter, not sell.

Impounded cars come back to the **home garage** once released.
