**Los Santos Customs**, **Benny's** and **Beeker's** take cars; the boat and air workshops take the rest. Drive in, and the menu opens.

- **Performance** — engine, brakes, transmission, turbo, suspension.
- **Cosmetics** — paint, wheels, liveries, neon, window tint, plates.
- **Armour** and **repairs**.
- **Time bomb** — a category of its own; see [Vehicle crime](wiki:crime/vehicle-crime#time-bomb).

Mods stay on the car through the garage and delivery. Prices go through the [discount](wiki:economy/bonuses-and-events) system.
