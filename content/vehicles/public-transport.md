## Metro

Buy a ticket at the machine on the platform ($5) and board. Riding without one is **fare evasion** — a star if you are caught.

## Taxis

Cabs on the ranks take you anywhere for a distance fare; `/taxi` calls one. Details on [Taxi driver](wiki:jobs/taxi#ai-taxis).

## Buses

Player-driven Dashound buses run the routes while somebody has the [job](wiki:jobs/side-jobs#bus-driver) — wait at a stop and press {key:INPUT_PICKUP} to board.
