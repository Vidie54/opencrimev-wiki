## Buying

Showrooms (car, boat, air) sell a rotating stock — what is on the floor changes on a schedule, so a model you want may be there next week rather than today. Test-drive it first. A bought vehicle gets a plate and lands in your **home garage**.

## My Vehicles

**Interaction Menu → My Vehicles** (`/vehicles`) lists every car by garage:

- **Deliver** — a mechanic brings it to you for **{money:mechanic.price}**.
- **Track** — a blip on the map.
- **Respawn** — a lost or stuck car goes back to its garage.
- **Move** it between your garages, and see what is **impounded**.

## Keys

Your cars are locked to you. {key:togglevehiclelock} locks and unlocks the one you are in or next to; `/lock` and `/unlock [id]` do the same from chat, and `/unlock <id>` gives another player the door.

## Fuel and damage

Cars burn fuel. Fill up at any petrol station (take the hose to the tank) or carry a jerry can. Damage persists: a wreck stays a wreck until you repair it at a [workshop](wiki:vehicles/customs).

## Losing it

Destroyed and abandoned cars go back to the garage on **Respawn**; cars an officer tows or impounds after an arrest are in the [impound](wiki:crime/prison-and-impound). The starter car cannot be sold; everything else can be, from the garage.
