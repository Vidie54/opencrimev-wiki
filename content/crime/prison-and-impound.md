## Being arrested

An arrest sends you to the cells for **{var:jail.baseSeconds} seconds plus {var:jail.secondsPerStar} per star**. Your **weapons are confiscated**; items and money stay, and the wanted level is cleared. `/jail` is an officer's command, not yours. Logging out in a cell pauses nothing — you finish the time when you come back.

## The impound

Cars near an arrest are impounded, and so are personal cars an officer tows. Get one back:

- **Bail** — {money:police.bail}, paid through an officer at the station.
- **Retrieve** — {money:police.retrieve} at the impound lot, no officer needed.

*My Vehicles → Impounded* lists what the lot holds.
