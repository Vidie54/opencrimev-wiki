## Store hold-up

Aim a weapon at the cashier of any 24/7, LTD or liquor store and hold it there while the bar fills. Friends speed it up — the take is split between everyone in the shop. Cash plus a chance of loot; the shop locks for three minutes afterwards, and the silent alarm calls the police.

## ATMs

Two ways in:

| | Pays | Stars | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shoot it open** | {money:atm.rewardMin}–{money:atm.rewardMax} | 1 | loud, fast |
| **Hack it** | {money:atm.hackMin}–{money:atm.hackMax} | {var:atm.hackWanted} | needs a *Hacking Device*; dispatch is called when you **start**; the device breaks |

Either way the ATM is out of service for {var:atm.cooldownMin} minutes, and a hacked ATM cannot be shot for a second payout.

## Security truck

A world event: an armoured truck with a route and guards. It takes at least two players — explosives on the rear doors, take the case, get away. Announced when it starts; see [Events](wiki:activities/events).

## Shipping containers

Ten to fifteen sealed containers are alive at any time, scattered around the docks and industrial areas — found by exploring, not by a blip. Press {key:INPUT_PICKUP} at the doors to open one; the loot inside is reserved for the opener for thirty seconds.
