## Pickpocketing

Crouch behind a walking NPC and hold {key:INPUT_PICKUP}, keeping pace for six seconds. Detection ramps up every tick; a quarter of marks fight back, and four in ten call the police. The take is $60–350 or a wallet, phone or watch for the pawn shop.

## Dumpster diving

Seven seconds at any dumpster, fifteen minutes before the same one refills. Mostly junk — which the [scrapyard](wiki:crime/vehicle-crime#scrapyard) buys — plus food, and now and then something valuable.

## The pawn shop

Buys valuables: watches, phones, jewellery, and the **encrypted USB drives** you find. Some items are worth more read than sold.

## Computers

Public desks around the city and the computer in a house or rental let you read **USB flash drives** and crack **encrypted drives** for cash.

## Scratch tickets and weed

Scratch tickets are sold at 24/7s — scratch them from the inventory. The **weed automat** sells joints that feed the drugs meter. Everything consumable is in [Inventory](wiki:economy/inventory).
