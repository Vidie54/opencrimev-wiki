## Placing one

`/bounty <id> <amount>` — at least **{money:bounty.min}**, plus a **{var:bounty.feePercent} % broker fee** you pay on top. One bounty per {var:bounty.cooldown} seconds. `/bounties` (also `/hitlist`) lists everyone with a price on their head.

## System bounties

Stealing a car puts an **anonymous bounty** on the thief a few seconds later — see [Vehicle crime](wiki:crime/vehicle-crime#carjacking).

## Collecting

Kill the target. The bounty is paid to the killer on the spot. It is still a murder as far as the police are concerned — the usual [stars](wiki:crime/wanted-level) apply.
