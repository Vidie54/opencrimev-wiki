## Carjacking

Steal a car — from the street or hotwire a parked one — and there is an 80 % chance of a star. Ten to fifteen seconds later an **anonymous bounty** of a few thousand dollars lands on your head, and there is a ten-minute cooldown before the next theft counts. Player-owned cars are locked; see [Owning a vehicle](wiki:vehicles/owning).

## Scrapyard

Drive a stolen car into the **crusher** at the scrapyard: cash plus scrap items, {var:scrap.xp} XP, once every {var:scrap.cooldownMin} minutes. The scrapyard also buys junk and scrap from the [inventory](wiki:economy/inventory).

## Vehicle export

A list of four wanted models refreshes every hour. Deliver one to the docks for the export price and 25 XP.

## Diversion into a warehouse

A stolen or towed car can be driven into your own **vehicle warehouse** instead of sold — it sits there as hot cargo until it cools off or you pay to clear it. See [Warehouses](wiki:properties/warehouses#cargo-from-jobs).

## Time bomb

Sold by the car workshops as a *Time bomb* upgrade. Only the installer can arm it: from the driver's seat with {key:INPUT_ATTACK}, then a fifteen-second fuse. The blast is treated like any other kill — stars, killfeed, bounties.
