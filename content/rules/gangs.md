Gang versus gang is legal content: no wanted level, no self-defence rule, no protection. Inside a gang the same applies. Joining one [ends New Player Protection for good](wiki:getting-started/new-player-protection).

## Turf

The warehouses are captured by standing in the zone — see [Gangs](wiki:jobs/gangs). Rules:

- No logging out inside a zone to hold it or to freeze a capture.
- No using passive mode or a protected activity (a race, the range) to sit in a zone untouchable.
- No blocking the zone with vehicles.

## Civilians

A gang is not a licence to shoot everybody. Killing non-gang players still earns stars and bounties, and repeatedly targeting one player is griefing regardless of colours.

## Police

Officers are not gang members. Working with a gang on duty is [corruption](wiki:rules/police).
