These are short on purpose. Breaking them costs a warning, a kick, a temporary ban or a permanent one — see [Punishments](wiki:rules/punishments).

## 1. No cheating

No mod menus, injectors, macros, aimbots, or exploits. **Report bugs, do not use them** — duplication, money glitches, interior or instance escapes and anything else the game clearly did not intend. Abusing a bug is treated the same as cheating.

## 2. One person, one account

No sharing accounts, no alternate accounts to dodge a ban. Evading a ban makes the new account a permanent one.

## 3. Names

Names must be readable. No impersonating staff, no slurs, no links or advertising in the name. See [First login](wiki:getting-started/first-login) for how names work — the name-change rule is part of this.

## 4. Chat and voice

No hate speech, no real-life threats, no doxxing, no spam, no advertising other servers. Trash talk between players is part of the game; anything that leaves the game is not.

## 5. Language

Chat is **English**. Use `/local` for anything else.

## 6. No farming, no dodging

- AFK farming, macro-looping jobs, and any other automation are out.
- Leaving the game to dodge consequences — combat logging while wanted, in jail, mid-arrest, mid-robbery — is punishable. The game keeps the state; you only annoy the people you were playing with.

## 7. Streamers

No stream sniping.

## 8. Staff decisions

Staff decisions are final in game. Argue on the Discord, not in chat. False reports waste everyone's time and are punishable too.
