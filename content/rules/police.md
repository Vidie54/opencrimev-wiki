Most of this the game does by itself — read it as "the game will". The rest is on the staff.

## The game will

- Give you a **wanted level** for *Excessive Force* if you kill a suspect who was not shooting at you, and for *Police Brutality* if you beat an innocent.
- Treat you as a **civilian off duty**: no arrests, no duty vehicle, no immunity.
- **Fine you** for killing an AI suspect instead of arresting them.
- **Log everything** the MDT COMMAND tab does: a kick or ban of an officer needs a reason, and the Chief who issued it is on record.
- Keep your career stats on the character — rank is earned, never assigned. See [Police](wiki:jobs/police#ranks).

## The staff will act on

- **Arrest farming** — arresting a friend who lets themselves be caught, over and over. Career stats get wiped and the job goes with them.
- **Duty kit off duty** — using the duty vehicle, armoury weapons or the uniform as a civilian.
- **MDT abuse** — kicking or banning officers without cause.
- **Corruption** — taking money to let somebody go, or working with a gang while on duty. Play the criminal if that is what you want; the badge is not for it.

Chief of Police is an office, not a rank: it goes to the highest-scoring officer online who has reached the top grade, and it changes hands as people come and go.
