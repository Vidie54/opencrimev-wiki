## The ladder

1. **Warning** — in chat or a private message.
2. **Kick** — reconnect and behave.
3. **Temporary ban** — hours to days, depending on the rule.
4. **Permanent ban** — cheating, ban evasion, and repeat offenders.

Cheating skips the ladder.

## Reporting

`/report [id] <reason>` sends a report to every staff member online and to the staff Discord channel. There is a cooldown, so make it count: the player id (from the player board, {key:+scoreboard}), what happened, and when. `/admins` shows who is online.

## Staff levels

| Level | Tag |
|---|---|
| Beta Tester | helps find bugs, no moderation powers |
| Helper | greets and guides new players |
| Moderator | warnings, kicks, temporary bans |
| Administrator | everything above plus permanent bans, refunds, investigations |
| Head Administrator | runs the server |

Staff names carry a colour tag in chat. Nobody on the staff will ever ask for your password or FiveM login.

## Appeals

Appeals go to the Discord ({discord}), in the ban-appeal channel. Include your name and the ban reason. Appeals in the general chat are ignored.
