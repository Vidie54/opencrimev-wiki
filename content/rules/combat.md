Killing other players is allowed and has in-game consequences: [wanted stars](wiki:crime/wanted-level), [drops](wiki:economy/money#dying), [bounties](wiki:crime/bounties). Rules exist only where the game cannot enforce fairness itself.

## No spawn-camping

Do not camp the hospitals or job start points waiting for people to appear. Somebody who has just respawned or just walked into the depot is not a target.

## No targeting one player

Killing somebody once is the game. Following one player around to ruin their session is griefing.

## New Player Protection is not a shield to bait from

Attacking others from behind it ends it anyway; doing it on purpose is a rule violation on top.

## Passive mode is not an escape hatch

Toggling passive to leave a fight you started is punishable. The game refuses it in combat; finding a way around that is the violation.

## Shops are neutral ground

Passive is forced inside shops, clothing stores and workshops. Shooting into or out of them, or abusing the edge of the zone, is griefing.

## Races and the shooting range are protected

Racers and shooters cannot be hit by outsiders — the game refuses the damage. Ramming the grid, blocking the track or camping the exit is griefing.

## Do not block the world with vehicles

Job vehicles, delivery points, garage doors, the impound gate, the metro — leave them usable.
