Press {key:interactionmenu} (or the middle mouse button) anywhere. This is the one menu the rest of the game hangs off.

## Sections

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Inventory** | the same as {key:open_inventory} — items, consumables, weapons |
| **Jobs & Missions** | who you work for, the job list, your active mission |
| **Animations** | emotes and scenarios (`/animations`) |
| **Vehicle Controls** | engine, lock, hood, trunk, doors, windows — also on {key:vehiclecontrolsmenu} inside a car |
| **My Vehicles** | every car you own, by garage. Deliver one to you for {money:mechanic.price}, track it on the map, respawn a lost one, see what is impounded (`/vehicles`) |
| **Properties** | houses, rentals, garages, warehouses you own or rent (`/properties`) |
| **Account Info** | statistics, Levels & XP, achievements *(coming soon)*, linked accounts (website / Discord), cooldowns (`/stats`) |
| **Leaderboards** | the server-wide top lists (`/top`) |
| **Options** | name change, HUD, radar, map blips, hints, units, passive mode, the wiki |
| **Kill Yourself** | respawn at a hospital when you are stuck — the [hospital fee](wiki:economy/money) applies |

## Options in detail

- **Change Name** — once per a fixed number of playtime hours. Your FiveM nickname has to match afterwards, or you cannot reconnect.
- **Display HUD / Mini-map Radar** — hide either; {key:+openHud} toggles the whole HUD.
- **Map Blips** — show or hide individual blips and whole categories.
- **Show Hints** — the rotating tips. Contextual "Press E" prompts stay.
- **Units** — speed and distance units.
- **Passive Mode** — see [New Player Protection](wiki:getting-started/new-player-protection#passive-mode-afterwards).
- **Open Wiki** — this.
