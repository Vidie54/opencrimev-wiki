## Read and accept the rules

Before character creation, a welcome dialog summarises the server rules. Choose **Read Server Rules Wiki** to read the full [general rules](wiki:rules/general) and the other rules pages. Close the wiki to return to the dialog, then choose **I Accept — Create Character** to continue.

## Choose your appearance

- **Gender** changes your character model and resets hair and starting clothes. Choose this first.
- **Dad Shape**, **Mom Shape** and **Resemblance** set your character's inherited appearance.
- **Hair Style** changes your starting haircut.
- **Top**, **Legs** and **Shoes** cycle through the available starting clothes.

Your name comes from the name registered during connection. The identity board displays that name; appearance settings do not rename your character. See [First login](wiki:getting-started/first-login) for name changes.

## Inspect your character

Hold the left mouse button outside the setup panel and drag sideways to rotate your character. Hover over a setting to bring that part of the body into view. The camera keeps its viewing direction when you rotate, so you can inspect the sides and back too.

Help messages explain the controls as you create your character. **Character Creation Wiki** opens this guide without losing your choices. Close the wiki to return to editing.

## Randomize or complete

**Randomize** changes parents, resemblance, hair and clothes for your selected gender. Keep adjusting any setting until you are happy with the result.

Choose **Complete Character** to review your decision. **Cancel** returns to editing. **Start Tutorial** begins the city introduction; **Skip Tutorial** goes straight to the arrival sequence.

Choose your inherited appearance carefully. Later clothing and barber visits offer their own appearance options; they are not the full first-login creator. See [Shops](wiki:economy/shops).

Kill and suicide commands are unavailable throughout setup, including the tutorial and arrival sequence.
