## On creation

Every new character starts with **{money:starter.spawnCash}** cash in the wallet. That is given at creation, whether or not you ever claim the rest.

## The claim

After **{var:starter.claimMinutes} minutes** of playtime you can claim the signing bonus at the **City Guide** (the airport blip). The claim gives, all at once:

| What | Details |
|---|---|
| Signing bonus | **{money:starter.signingBonus}** straight to the bank |
| Weapons | a pistol and a micro SMG, 100 rounds each |
| Armour | full body armour |
| Garage voucher | one free **{var:starter.garageSlots}-slot land garage** of your choice |
| Starter car | one of: Annis Elegy RH8, Karin Sultan, Bravado Buffalo |

The car is delivered when you redeem the garage voucher, parked in that garage. It is yours to keep and drive, but it **cannot be sold** — it was free.

> [!WARNING] Claim it early
> The pack is for new characters. Once your playtime passes the claim window it is gone for good, so the City Guide is worth the walk on day one.
