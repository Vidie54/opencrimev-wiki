## What it is

For your first **{var:newplayer.hours} hours of playtime** other players cannot damage you and you cannot damage them. It counts online time, not calendar time, so a break does not use it up.

Protected players are listed with `/newplayers` (also `/passive`, `/protection`), and their name tag on the player board says so.

## What ends it early

- **Attacking other players.** A hit on somebody counts as a strike; after {var:newplayer.strikes} strikes the protection is revoked. Shooting from behind the shield is exactly what it is not for.
- **Joining a gang** removes it for good. Gang life is PvP by definition — see [Gangs](wiki:jobs/gangs).

## Passive mode afterwards

Once the protection is over you can switch **Passive Mode** on yourself: **Interaction Menu → Options → Passive Mode**. Passive players cannot be hurt by other players and cannot use weapons.

The server decides, not the checkbox:

- refused while New Player Protection is still running (you are already safe, and passive would let you drop the protection flag)
- refused in combat — you cannot toggle it to escape a fight you started
- forced on inside shops, clothing stores and workshops

> [!WARNING]
> Toggling passive to dodge consequences is a [rule violation](wiki:rules/combat), not a trick.
