## Connecting

Find the server in the FiveM browser or connect through the Discord's invite link. There is no whitelist and no application — the first connection makes your account.

## Your name is your FiveM name

Your character is named after your **FiveM nickname** the first time you join. The two have to match: if you later rename yourself in FiveM, the server refuses the connection until they agree again, so treat the name as fixed.

You can change it in game once per a set number of playtime hours: **Interaction Menu → Options → Change Name**. Change the FiveM nickname to match straight after. Names must be readable, must not impersonate staff and must follow the [rules](wiki:rules/general#3-names).

## Character creator

A new account goes through the character creator: sex, heritage, face, hair, clothes. Nothing here is final — the barber, clothing stores and the wardrobe in a house can change all of it later. See [Shops](wiki:economy/shops).

## The tutorial and the spawn

After the creator a short flyover tutorial shows the city and the main systems. You spawn at **Los Santos International Airport** with {money:starter.spawnCash} in cash.

> [!TIP] Getting into town
> The cabs on the rank outside the terminal are free to board — walk up to a passenger door and press {key:INPUT_PICKUP}, then pick a destination. Any unlocked car offers a passenger seat the same way, including other players' cars.
