Buyable storage on the **warehouse** blip, per account, no expiry. Two kinds:

## Vehicle warehouse

A garage with **tiers**: buy the small one, upgrade for more slots. It appears in *My Vehicles* like any garage and takes any vehicle its class allows.

## Goods warehouse

Volume storage for items, by **class** (low / mid / high value) in **Small / Medium / Large** tiers. Walk in, and the shelves show what is stored; the desk moves items in and out. Upgrading empties nothing — the next visit is simply the bigger box.

## Cargo from jobs

A [truck driver](wiki:jobs/side-jobs#truck-driver) with a goods warehouse, or a [tow driver](wiki:jobs/side-jobs#tow-truck) / [Simeon repo man](wiki:activities/simeon-contracts) with a vehicle warehouse, can **divert** the load there instead of being paid: drive to the warehouse's drop point with the cargo. Cargo:

- shares the tier's capacity,
- is **sold from the desk** — the reason to do it is a better price than the job pays,
- a **hot car** has to cool off for a while first, or you pay a clearing fee,
- blocks selling the warehouse until it is gone.

There is a cooldown per job between diversions and a cap on unsold value.

## Selling the warehouse

Pays back a share of the undiscounted price; refused while anything is stored.
