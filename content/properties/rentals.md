Rentals are not unique — every tenant gets their own copy of the room. Rent by the **term of {var:rentals.termHours} hours**, and bank up to **{var:rentals.maxBankedDays} days** ahead.

| Room | Per term |
|---|---|
| Motel room | $1,500 |
| Apartment | $4,000 |
| Hotel suite | $9,000 |
| Penthouse | $25,000 |

Inside: wardrobe, weapon storage and a computer, like a house. **Manage Room** at the door extends the lease; when it runs out the room is locked until you pay again — nothing inside is lost.
