A house has **one owner server-wide**. When it is sold, it is on the market again.

## Prices

| Size | Base price |
|---|---|
| Small | $85,000 |
| Medium | $220,000 |
| Large | $550,000 |
| Mansion | $1,500,000 |

The zone multiplies the base: Vinewood Hills costs more than Rancho for the same size. The price is on the house's marker.

## Inside

- **Wardrobe** — saved outfits, and the job kits you have earned.
- **Weapon storage** — twenty weapons that survive your death.
- **Armoury** and **computer**.
- **Garage** — a parking spot per size.

## Living there

You can spawn at home, invite players in (`/invite`), and manage the house from **Interaction Menu → Properties**. Selling pays back a share of the price, and empties the storage — take what you want out first.
