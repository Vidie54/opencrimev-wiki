## Wardrobe

In every house and rental: **save**, **load** and **rename** outfits. Clothes come from the clothing stores; the wardrobe only remembers combinations.

## Uniforms

Job kits are separate from bought clothes:

- A **locker** at a station or hospital issues the job's uniforms and takes them back. The first uniform stashes your own clothes; *Own Clothes* restores them.
- The **wardrobe** also lists the kits you have **earned for good** — an ex-officer keeps the kits their rank unlocked.
- Wearing a uniform gives no authority. A civilian in a police kit is a civilian.

## Weapon storage

Twenty weapons per property. Stored weapons survive your death and your arrests. The **armoury** in a house is the bench for it.
