Every side job starts the same way: go to the depot, get into the job vehicle, press {key:INPUT_PICKUP}. `/cancelmission` ends the run, `/quitjob` leaves the job.

## Pizza delivery

Three **Pizza This!** shops. Grab the scooter, deliver to the marked address before the timer runs out — a fast delivery earns a tip on top of {money:jobs.pizzaboy.salary}.

## Taco seller

Take the `taco` van from the depot and park it anywhere. NPC customers walk up and buy; you are paid **per taco**, not per shift. Close the stand to end the run.

## Bus driver

The **Dashound** depot. Follow the route, stop at every stop, let people on and off. {money:jobs.busdriver.salary} per route, with XP per stop.

## Truck driver

The **Port of Los Santos**: a Hauler, Phantom or Packer, a trailer to hitch, a long haul across the map. {money:jobs.truckdriver.salary} per delivery.

> [!TIP] Diversion
> Own a goods warehouse? Once the cargo is secured you can unload it there instead of being paid, and sell it later from the warehouse desk. See [Warehouses](wiki:properties/warehouses#cargo-from-jobs).

## Tow truck

Starts at the **impound lot**. Hook the abandoned car you are sent to and bring it in. {money:jobs.towtruck.salary} per car. A tow driver who owns a vehicle warehouse can divert a hooked car there as a hot vehicle.

## Firefighter

The **fire station**. Take the engine to the fire and put it out. {money:jobs.firefighter.salary} per call.
