Four gangs: **Vagos**, **Ballas**, **Marabunta Grande** and **The Lost MC**. Join at the gang's marker; `/leavegang` leaves. There is no salary — the income is turf.

> [!WARNING]
> Joining a gang removes [New Player Protection](wiki:getting-started/new-player-protection) permanently, and gang fights carry no wanted level and no self-defence rule.

## Turf warehouses

| Warehouse | Capture | Cooldown after a failed defence |
|---|---|---|
| Docks Weapons Cache | 10 min | 1 h |
| Cypress Flats Weed Warehouse | 5 min | 30 min |
| Paleto Drug Lab | 3 min | 15 min |

Stand in the zone to capture it; the bar fills while your gang holds the ground and drains while the defenders do. A held warehouse **produces** on a tick, and the gang collects the output. The gang that owns each warehouse is visible on the map.

Rules for turf are on [Gang rules](wiki:rules/gangs).
