## Earning XP

Every completed job run, arrest, mission, race and range session pays XP. The amounts are on each job's page and in the [Jobs overview](wiki:jobs/overview).

## Levelling up

Each level pays **{money:level.cash}** cash. Milestones:

| Level | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| {var:jobs.police.level} | the [Police](wiki:jobs/police) job |

Your level and the XP bar are on the HUD; the full curve and your history are under **Interaction Menu → Account Info → Levels & XP**. `/top` shows the server leaderboards.
