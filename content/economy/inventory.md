Open it with {key:open_inventory} or from the interaction menu.

## Categories

- **Consumables** — food, drink, alcohol, joints; using one plays the animation and feeds the matching [need](wiki:getting-started/hud-and-keys#needs).
- **Junk** — what dumpsters give; the scrapyard buys it.
- **Valuables** — wallets, phones, watches, jewellery; the pawn shop buys them.
- **Collectibles** and **Misc** — USB drives, hacking devices, scratch tickets, keys.

## Using, giving, dropping

Select an item to use it, give it to a nearby player, or drop it on the ground. Using something takes a moment and locks other actions while the animation plays.

## Weapons

Weapons are separate from items. You keep them until you die — then one per group drops — or until you are jailed. A house or rental has a **weapon storage** for twenty weapons; see [Wardrobe & storage](wiki:properties/wardrobe-and-storage).
