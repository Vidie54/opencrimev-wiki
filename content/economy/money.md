## Cash and bank

You carry **cash** and keep the rest in the **bank**. Shops, jobs and most prices work with either, but only cash is at risk when you die — bank the rest.

ATMs and bank branches do deposits, withdrawals, transfers to another player and show your history. Every ATM on the map is usable (and [robbable](wiki:crime/robberies#atms)).

## Dying

When you die:

- **{var:drops.percent} % of your cash** falls on the ground in piles anybody can pick up. The piles vanish after five minutes.
- **One weapon per weapon group** drops beside you — your best pistol, your best rifle, and so on. The rest is gone.
- A **hospital fee of {money:respawn.deathFee}** is charged when you respawn, from the bank if the cash is not there.

The death screen fills for {var:respawn.deathTime} seconds before you can respawn; a [paramedic](wiki:jobs/paramedic) can revive you where you fell for no fee. Wanted stars survive death. Details: [FAQ](wiki:faq).

## Bonuses

Payouts are multiplied by active [events and VIP](wiki:economy/bonuses-and-events). Prices in shops go through the same system in the other direction.
